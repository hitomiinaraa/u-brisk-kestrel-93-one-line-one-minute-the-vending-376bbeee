Entry for the [make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-brisk-kestrel-93-one-line-one-minute-the-vending-376bbeee): one pickup line, spoken by a vending machine to a passing human.

> No exact change required, I take IOUs in smiles, and yours just jammed my coin slot.

The line is the machine itself talking: it flips its own exact-change rule, takes payment in smiles, and the punch lands on the last words when that smile jams its coin slot. Short, speakable, and charming rather than creepy.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

Writes the entry to `/output/entry.txt` and prints it to stdout.
